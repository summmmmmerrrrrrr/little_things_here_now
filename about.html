about the project

little_things_here_now is an ongoing collection of ordinary moments that briefly interrupt the way we move through everyday life.

Sometimes the ordinary world seems to perform without being asked: a curtain escapes through a window, a chicken waves from a parked car, and an improvised gate finds its own peculiar shape. These small encounters are easy to miss, but once noticed, they can make an ordinary place feel briefly strange, funny, or unexpectedly alive.

The project collects these encounters and transforms some of them into images, films, small interventions and invitations to participate. This website is both a container for those things and a small intervention of its own.

why do we need a stronger sense of now, now?

this project began in switzerland, where everyday life often feels carefully organised, efficient and quietly predictable. routes are calculated, decisions are anticipated, interfaces remove friction, and systems guide us from one action to the next. this efficiency is useful, but when everything works as expected, we can move through daily life without fully noticing where we are.

in this project, now does not simply mean the present time. it describes a brief moment of conscious presence: a small interruption in which someone becomes aware, “i am here, and this is happening now.”

these moments cannot always be planned. they may begin with a mistake, an improvised solution, an unexpected object, or something ordinary behaving strangely. such encounters briefly interrupt the smoothness of a system and invite us to look again.

this is why now matters now. the project is not against order, efficiency or technology. it asks what might be lost when life becomes too seamless—and whether small moments of friction, imperfection and surprise can return a little awareness and agency to everyday experience.

first, and perhaps most importantly: who am i?

my name is yunshu qin, people also call me summer. i am someone who likes walking without a destination, noticing little things, and taking photos before i have decided whether they are important.

i began with what was already around me. i walked, looked and collected. i photographed small encounters in everyday spaces: things that seemed slightly out of place, improvised, accidentally funny, or briefly alive.

i turned some of these encounters into images and a short film. rather than trying to explain everything immediately, i wanted to hold on to the moment when something ordinary first becomes strange enough to notice.

i also made small interventions and invitations. some ask people to look differently, some introduce a minor disruption into an ordinary situation, and some leave space for other people to add their own observations.

this website brings these parts together. it is an archive of collected moments, a place to share interventions, and an invitation to notice or take away a little thing. it is also an intervention itself: a small digital space that asks visitors to slow down, look again, and see what happens.
