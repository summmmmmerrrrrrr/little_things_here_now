# little things here now 🪶

A little website about noticing little things.

This website started as part of my MA graduation project, *Designing the Sense of “NOW”*, at Basel Academy of Art and Design FHNW.

I've been thinking about how much of our everyday life happens on autopilot. We follow routines, click buttons, take the same routes, and sometimes forget to actually look at what's around us.

So I started collecting little moments, trying things out, and making small interventions in familiar places.

Eventually, some of these experiments found their way into this website.

It's not really designed to be explored in a particular order. You can click around, follow a feather, get distracted, or try to catch a pigeon. Some things might make sense, some might not. That's kind of the point.

I wanted to make a little space where curiosity could lead the way, and where getting distracted might actually be a good thing.

### A little bit about making it

I designed and built this website using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with the help of Claude along the way.

I'm a visual designer, not a developer, so a lot of the process involved experimenting, figuring things out, breaking things, fixing them, and occasionally wondering why something that worked five minutes ago had suddenly stopped working.

But it was fun. And I learned a lot.
